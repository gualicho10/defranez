00Introduction

Modeling + Render

This project explores the integration of a single-family home within the rugged landscape of Patagonia. The primary challenge was to translate the warmth of noble materials—such as wood and stone—into a contemporary architectural language that respects the scale of the surroundings.

We focused on a simple yet efficient volumetric design, prioritizing natural light intake and panoramic views of the mountain range. This presentation details the technical journey from initial drafting to final construction.

_ PROJECTHouse in the mountains
_ LOCATIONChubut, Patagonia, Argentina
_ REQUESTSketchup + V-Ray/Enscape + Photoshop
_ YEAR2025
01Project

Development of comprehensive technical documentation, including architectural floor plans and longitudinal sections to define internal spatiality. The design utilizes a modular grid system to optimize construction resources in remote geographical locations.

02Modeling

The 3D modeling phase was executed with technical precision, transforming architectural schemes into a detailed digital structure. Special emphasis was placed on the structural accuracy of the timber joints and the site’s topography to ensure a realistic environment.

Through wireframes and volumetric models, we validated proportions and solar incidence on the facades, allowing for dynamic adjustments before moving into the texturing and post-processing stages.

03Renders

Photorealistic visualization designed to capture the atmosphere of the retreat under various lighting conditions. Global illumination was carefully tuned to highlight natural textures and the contrast between interior warmth and the exterior climate.

04Results

The transition from the digital model to the physical build demonstrates the viability of the proposed design. On-site monitoring allowed us to compare the 3D model’s precision with traditional construction techniques applied in the region.
