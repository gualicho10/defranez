A bit about me

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 22 years of experience — started in 2004 with a non-stop journey across Press, Editorial, Web, Gaming, and Mobile. I've worked on-site for major companies and remotely with startups, as well as a freelancer across LATAM, USA, and Israel.

THE PROJECT

A Web3 gaming marketplace
built from scratch.

Open Loot is a crypto-gaming marketplace built by Big Time Studios — the infrastructure layer for Web3 gaming, enabling players to buy, sell, and trade in-game NFT assets across multiple partner titles.

As sole Lead Product Designer (2022–2026), I owned end-to-end design: from UX strategy and wireframes through UI design, design system, prototyping, and ongoing iteration with engineering.

Scope included:
homepage, marketplace, game discovery, OL Chain explorer, user wallet, token dashboards, onboarding, and responsive mobile layouts.

MY ROLE

End-to-end

design process.

01

UX Strategy & Research

User flows, information architecture, persona definition (casual player vs. advanced trader), competitive analysis, heuristic evaluation.

02

Visual Design & UI

Full V2 redesign — new visual language, dark-mode system, component design, brand alignment across all platform surfaces.

03

Design System

Token-based Figma system: color tokens, typography scale, spacing, icon set, component library with all interactive states.

04

Prototyping

High-fidelity interactive Figma prototypes for stakeholder review, usability testing, and engineering handoff.

05

Mobile Design

Responsive layouts and mobile-first screens for marketplace, game discovery, and onboarding flows.

06

Dev Collaboration & QA

Figma specs, annotations, edge-case docs. QA of implemented screens, visual regression tracking.

KEY DESIGN CHALLENGES
01

Web3 UX for Mainstream Gamers

Abstracting blockchain complexity — wallet connection, gas-free transactions, NFT ownership — into familiar gaming UI patterns without losing trustworthiness for crypto-native users.

02

Multi-Game Data Complexity

Presenting NFT items from 6+ games with different art styles, rarity systems, and metadata structures — all within a single cohesive marketplace UI that feels natural to each game's audience.

03

Single Designer, Full Platform

Operating as the sole designer across the entire product surface required strong prioritization, a robust design system, and tight engineering collaboration to scale output efficiently over 4 years.

WHAT GOT SHIPPED

Outcomes &

impact.

Full V2 Launch

Complete redesign and launch of openloot.com V2 — new visual language, improved IA, and a scalable component system deployed to production.

OL Chain Integration

Designed all user-facing surfaces for Open Loot's native blockchain — transaction explorer, token dashboards, and in-platform blockchain activity feeds.

6 Partner Games

Onboarded and designed for: Big Time, Boss Fighters, World Shards, Reign of Terror, Shatterpoint, and Desolation — each with custom marketplace integration.

Mobile Responsive

Delivered responsive designs across all major breakpoints, enabling the full marketplace and game discovery experience on mobile without a native app.

Living Design System

Built and maintained a Figma design system that scaled from launch through 4 years of product iteration — a single source of truth for the entire UI.

Rapid Iteration

Established a design-to-dev workflow reducing handoff friction, enabling fast concept-to-implementation cycles in a fast-paced Web3 startup environment.
